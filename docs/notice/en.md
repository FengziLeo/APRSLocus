# v2.1.0 is out · APRSLocus enters a maintenance-first, stable phase

v2.1.0 has been released. This one ties up the loose ends — the feature set is now where we want it.

From this release on, APRSLocus moves into a **maintenance-first, stable phase**: no more ground-up rewrites. The effort goes into keeping it steady and correct — protocols stay current, devices stay recognised, edge cases get an owner, and every report you send gets an answer. Smaller steps, always forward.

## 🥚 A small easter egg in 2.1.0: tap, and the planet turns around

The egg is on the **About** page — the spot you most likely pass every day.

That slowly turning star on the cover is not a picture. **Give it a light tap**: it shudders, turns around, and drops you into a page of story. There is a planet turning there too, a number that keeps climbing, people who keep watch on the airwaves — and the people who built this thing alongside them.

There is sound on that page; turn your speakers up. No long press, no five-second wait — one tap and the story arrives. Changed your mind? One more tap turns the cover back, and the story stays closed.

The rest is yours to see.

## 🌐 A Chinese reference site: aprsnet.cn

The Chinese notes gathered while building this client now have a home of their own: **[aprsnet.cn](https://aprsnet.cn/)**.

It explains what APRS is, how to fill in beacon settings, and what each part of a packet means. It also carries an [official TOCALL lookup](https://aprsnet.cn/tocall.html): paste a whole packet line and see what the other station is running. The Chinese APRS-IS documentation is [collected there](https://aprsnet.cn/docs.html) too.

Both the site and the app are kept by the same author. The site stands on its own: it holds the Chinese reference material and the lookup tools, while app updates still come from here.
